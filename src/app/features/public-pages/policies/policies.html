3. Privacy Policy

As a general rule, this website does not collect personal information unless you voluntarily provide it.

Site Visit Data

This website records visitor information for statistical purposes, including server address, browser type, date and time of access, pages visited, downloaded documents, and referring websites.

Cookies

A cookie is a small piece of software code sent to your browser by a website. This website does not use tracking cookies.

Email Management

Your email address is recorded only if you choose to send a message. It will be used solely for the purpose for which it was provided and will not be disclosed without your consent.

Collection of Personal Information

If personal information is requested, you will be informed how it will be used. If you believe these privacy principles have not been followed, please contact the website administrator.

Note: The use of the term "Personal Information" in this privacy statement refers to any information from which your identity is apparent or can be reasonably ascertained.
