Agnipath Scheme
Official Framework

A transformative national policy to engage youth in the Armed Forces for a 4-year tenure, fostering discipline, advanced technical skills, and leadership.

Program Pillars

The Agnipath scheme provides motivated youth with intensive technical and combat training. Upon completion of their tenure, Agniveers emerge equipped with high-value professional certifications and discipline.

✦

Four-Year Tenure

A dedicated engagement in the Armed Forces with rigorous skill and behavioral training.

✦

Seva Nidhi Package

A financial corpus of approximately ₹11.71 Lakh granted upon completion of the engagement period.

✦

Skill Certification & Mapping

Kaushal Praman Patra through NCVET and NSQF certification recognized across public and private sectors.

Enrollment Standards

01

Recruitment open for all eligible Indian citizens between 17.5 to 21 years.

02

Selection governed by transparent physical, medical, and written criteria.

03

Specialized technical training imparted at premier regimental training centers.

Career Transition Linkage

The Ministry of Home Affairs provides reserved vacancies and age relaxation in CAPFs (BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles) and State Police forces.
